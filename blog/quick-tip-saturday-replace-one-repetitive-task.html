Quick Tip Saturday: Replace One Repetitive Task (Jan 24, 2026)

January 24, 2026 at 7:44 AM · Stottly Enterprises

Written by Daniel J. Stott, Founder of Stottly Enterprises — 20+ years across enterprise technology, retail, live production, and consulting.

If you want a fast win with AI, do not start with strategy decks or big transformations.

Start here.

The One Question That Works

Ask yourself:

What is one task I do every week that I do not need to personally think about anymore?

Not the most complex task.

Not the most important.

The most repetitive.

Why This Works

Replacing one repetitive task does three things immediately:

It proves AI can be trusted

It creates visible time savings

It builds confidence to go further

Momentum matters more than sophistication early on.

Examples That Actually Stick

Good candidates:

Drafting the same weekly report

Summarizing meetings or notes

Sorting incoming requests

Preparing first drafts or outlines

Drafting the same weekly report

Summarizing meetings or notes

Bad candidates:

High judgment decisions

One off creative work

Anything without a clear pattern

High judgment decisions

One off creative work

The Takeaway

Do not try to automate your job.

Replace one small, repeatable task and let the result earn the next step.
