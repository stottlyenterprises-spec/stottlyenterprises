In Case You Missed It. In Case You Missed It, The Weekly Wrap (Oct 12, 2025)

October 12, 2025 at 10:10 AM · Stottly Enterprises

We’re excited to announce a change. From now on, StottifAI will publish a fresh blog post each day. We’re shifting from a few posts per week to daily insights in AI, business strategy, human and tech, and hands-on ideas because innovation doesn’t wait. Expect concise, high-value posts you can read over coffee that help you stay ahead — and of course, we’ll continue to bring you breaking AI news.

So onward with our first official Weekly Wrap!

This week reinforces a shift: AI is becoming foundational infrastructure, not just flashy experiments. If your business dreams of smarter automation, more responsive systems, or better insights, this is your window. Start asking not if you integrate AI, but how to do it in ways that amplify your strengths rather than disrupt them.

Stay tuned tomorrow for your first daily post under the new rhythm, and thank you for being part of this journey.

OpenAI and AMD Ink Massive Chip Supply Deal

OpenAI struck a multi-year agreement with AMD to deploy six gigawatts of its Instinct MI450 GPUs, giving AMD a strong seat in AI’s hardware race. OpenAI will also receive a warrant to acquire up to 160 million shares in AMD, conditional on deployment milestones.

IBM Expands Agentic AI with New Agent Catalog

IBM is pushing deeper into enterprise AI with its Agent Catalog, part of the watsonx Orchestrate ecosystem. Businesses can now access and deploy prebuilt agents that streamline workflows across HR, procurement, and sales.

Agents Built by Anyone: No Code AI in IBM Orchestrate

IBM’s latest updates include a no code Agent Builder, enabling nontechnical users to spin up agents in minutes. This democratization of AI lowers

barriers for teams to adopt agentic tools without deep engineering.
