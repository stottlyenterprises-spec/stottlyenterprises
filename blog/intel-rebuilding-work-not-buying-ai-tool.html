Intel Is Rebuilding Work, Not Buying Another AI Tool

Published July 24, 2026 at 6:23 AM · Stottly Enterprises

Intel and Google Cloud announced an expanded collaboration on July 16 to deploy Gemini Enterprise across engineering, supply chain, and corporate operations. The predictable reading is that another large company is adopting artificial intelligence. The more useful reading is that Intel is using AI as an excuse to redesign how work moves across a complicated enterprise.

Most businesses do not have Intel's scale, but they have the same structural problem. Information is divided across email, documents, systems, and experienced employees. Adding AI to that environment can produce faster fragments without producing a faster business. The correct starting point is a workflow with measurable friction: identify where time is lost, what evidence a decision requires, and who owns the outcome.

Controls must be designed into the same workflow. Sensitive information should have clear access rules, recommendations should expose their evidence, and high-consequence decisions should retain human review. Adoption also depends on managers: employees need to know which work should change and what they remain accountable for.

Businesses should not ask how quickly they can buy the same class of tool. They should ask which part of their operating model is ready to become measurably better.
