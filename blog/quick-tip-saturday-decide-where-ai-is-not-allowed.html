Quick Tip Saturday: Decide Where AI Is Not Allowed (Jan 31, 2026)

January 31, 2026 at 9:10 AM · Stottly Enterprises

Here’s a quick AI win most teams overlook:

Before deciding where AI should be used, decide where it is not allowed.

Why This Works

AI is powerful precisely because it is flexible.

That flexibility becomes a problem when there are no boundaries.

Clear exclusions do three things immediately:

Reduce second guessing

Protect high judgment decisions

Speed up everything else

Reduce second guessing

Protect high judgment decisions

Speed up everything else

Constraints create confidence.

What to Exclude on Purpose

Good candidates for exclusion:

Final accountability decisions

Performance reviews

Sensitive people issues

One off creative judgment calls

These areas benefit from AI input, not AI authority.

What This Unlocks

Once boundaries are clear, teams stop debating every use case.

AI becomes a support system instead of a question mark.

Work speeds up because judgment is protected, not diluted.

The Takeaway

AI adoption accelerates fastest when people know where it stops.

Define the line once.

Everything else gets easier.
