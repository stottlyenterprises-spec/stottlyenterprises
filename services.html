HOW WE WORK

Six steps, every engagement.

01 · Observe

Watch how work actually happens before assuming what the problem is.

02 · Listen

Talk to the people doing the work, not just the people managing it.

03 · Question

Ask until a real pattern emerges rather than a convenient one.

04 · Understand

Confirm the real problem before proposing any solution.

05 · Build

Design and implement only once the problem is the right one.

06 · Iterate

Adjust based on what actually happens, not what the plan assumed would happen.
