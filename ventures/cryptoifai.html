How it works

Three engines. One disciplined loop.

01 · LEARN

The Modeler

Trains a pattern memory for each coin across seven timeframes, from hourly moves to weekly trends. It refreshes on a regular cycle, so the system is never trading on stale learning.

02 · ANALYZE

The Analyst

Watches live market data around the clock, matching current conditions against trained memory to produce a conviction signal: a graded read on how strongly the data supports an entry.

03 · EXECUTE

The Executor

Turns signals into orders only after every risk check passes. Sizes positions, averages in on confirmed dips, locks in gains with a trailing exit, and journals a plain-English reason for every trade.

The part that matters

Risk management first. Everything else second.

Most trading bots are an entry signal with a dream. CryptoifAI was built in the opposite order: the safeguards came first, and every one of them is enforced in code on every single tick.

Hard stop-lossEvery position has a firm exit floor. No exceptions, no averaging into oblivion.
Trailing profit lockGains arm a trailing exit that follows the peak up, and it never gives ground back.
Time-stopPositions that stall too long are closed. Capital doesn't get held hostage.
Disciplined DCA ladderAverages down only at defined levels or on a strong signal, capped per day and per position.
Kill switchA drawdown circuit breaker that can halt all new buying automatically.
Cash reserveA fixed share of the account is never spent, regardless of any signal.
Price sanity guardCross-checks two independent market feeds before buying, so a bad quote never triggers a trade.
Hard scope limitTrades crypto cash only. Structurally unable to touch stocks, options, or other holdings.
