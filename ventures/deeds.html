D.E.E.D.S.

Progress, Not Perfection. Stottly Enterprises' first App Store app: a private personal operating system for tasks, goals, health, relationships, business, calendar, and reflection.

First App Store Release · Alpha

A calm command center for your whole life.

D.E.E.D.S. is Stottly Enterprises' first App Store app: a private personal operating system that brings tasks, goals, health, relationships, business, calendar, and reflection into one place. It's built around a simple idea: steady, honest progress beats chasing an impossible standard.

It's currently in alpha with a small group of early users, ahead of a public App Store launch targeted for August 2026.

The Framework

What D.E.E.D.S. stands for.

Five plain-language stages that describe how real progress happens. This is the framework the app is organized around.

D

Detect

Notice where you actually are right now, across the parts of life that matter to you.

E

Explore

Look at your options honestly before committing to a direction.

E

Enable

Set up the small structures and habits that make progress possible.

D

Drive

Take consistent action, one deliberate step at a time.

S

Sustain

Keep it going: reflect, adjust, and protect the progress you've made.
