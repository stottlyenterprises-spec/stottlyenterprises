Flagship Project

Restaurant Operations Platform

Turning 12 locations of paper-based operations into one digital, bilingual system.

The Problem

The franchise relied on paper-based operational books and manual reporting across 12 locations. Managers completed food safety and compliance tasks inconsistently, and information was hard to share across locations. Communication was fragmented between English-only and Spanish-only managers, and administrative work kept managers in the office instead of on the floor.

Discovery

Rather than gathering requirements from a conference room, I spent a week observing four managers in their normal environment, interviewed every manager who had time to speak with me, and mapped the existing paper workflows. I designed the platform around how managers already worked, instead of forcing them into a new process.

The Solution
  • Standardized Checklists. Consistent daily operational, food safety, and compliance workflows across every location.
  • Centralized Communication. One system for managers instead of fragmented, location-by-location updates.
  • Written Translation. Manager-to-manager messages in the platform were translated between English and Spanish in real time, so language was never a barrier to consistency.
  • Live Spoken Translation. A built-in speech translation tool let managers communicate on the spot with team members or customers speaking a different language, no separate app or delay required.
  • Reduced Admin Burden. Less time on paperwork, more time on the floor coaching employees and serving guests.

Results

12Restaurant locations supported
~50Managers using the platform
+15%Increase in completion rates
9,000+Management hours returned annually

The math behind the headline number: roughly 50 managers × 30 minutes saved per day, conservatively, × 360 operating days a year ≈ 9,000 hours returned annually. Beyond the numbers: managers spend more time on the floor with employees and customers.

Enterprise Case Study

Apple Business Messages

Building the operational foundation for a brand-new global support channel from scratch.

The Challenge

Apple was launching Business Messages as an entirely new customer support channel. There were no established advisor methodologies, communication standards, or best practices for supporting customers through asynchronous messaging. Advisors handled far more simultaneous conversations than traditional phone support, creating new operational challenges no one had solved before.

My Role

I was selected as part of the early team responsible for building the operational foundation of the new channel. I developed advisor workflows, conversation methodology, style guides, process documentation, presentations, and training materials that standardized how advisors worked within the platform from day one.

The Chat Flow Methodology

The conversation framework I built and taught so advisors could move every chat from greeting to resolution consistently, at scale. As advisor workloads increased, I also helped develop a phrase library tool that cut repetitive typing and reduced handle time. Both became part of AppleCare's training curriculum.

Results

8,000+Employees supported worldwide as the channel scaled
1.5 minReduction in average handle time
10%Improvement in CSAT scores
15+Leadership Academy classes shaped by direct feedback
Founder Case Study

Black Gryphon Productions

Building and running an independent theater company from the ground up for a decade, before "operations" was ever in my title.

The Challenge

Independent theater runs on almost no margin for error: tight budgets, small and often volunteer-heavy crews, and opening-night deadlines that never move. There was no operational playbook to run a company like this sustainably. Every system, from budgeting to casting to crew scheduling, had to be built from scratch and rebuilt every production cycle.

My Role

As founder and director, I owned every operational layer of the company: budgets, vendor relationships, scheduling, and production management, on top of directing the creative work itself, regularly coordinating casts of up to 25 performers alongside technical, design, and operational teams. I built it while working full-time elsewhere to fund it. There was no outside investment, no safety net, just whatever the day job covered.

Production Systems & Rehearsal Workflows

I built repeatable production systems and rehearsal workflows that let small teams consistently deliver work that looked and felt far larger than the budget behind it. It's the same instinct I bring to operational consulting today: build the system once, and the results become repeatable instead of heroic.

Results

10Years running an independent theater company
25Performers coordinated per production, plus crew

Route Optimization

Bakery Route Optimization

The owner ran five overlapping delivery routes that had developed organically, with redundant travel and inconsistent territory assignments. I reviewed the routes, analyzed customer distribution, and redesigned the structure, consolidating 5 routes down to 3 and eliminating two entirely.
