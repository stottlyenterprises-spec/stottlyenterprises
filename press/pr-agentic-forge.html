Stottly Enterprises Targets Fourth Quarter 2026 for Agentic Forge 1.0

A business operating system built to run project management, team communication, company memory, and a governed AI workforce from a single company record.

For Immediate Release

Agentic Forge is on track for a fourth quarter 2026 release, with the preview build already available to early users.

ORLANDO, Fla., July 24, 2026. Stottly Enterprises today confirmed that Agentic Forge, its business operating system venture, is targeting a fourth quarter 2026 launch for version 1.0. The preview build is live now, and the team is using that early usage to shape what ships at launch.

Agentic Forge is built to replace the patchwork of tools most companies run on: project management software, team chat, document tools, a CRM, and a growing list of disconnected AI point solutions, with one coordinated workspace. Instead of stitching those systems together, a founder or operator works from a single company record that holds the business plan, the active work, the team, and the history behind every decision.

The system is organized around a persistent Project Manager, human and AI employees working inside the same accountable structure, and a company memory that carries decisions and context forward into future work. Permissioned integrations let Agentic Forge coordinate with the tools a company already uses instead of asking them to abandon everything at once.

"We set the fourth quarter target because that's what the preview build has told us is realistic," said Daniel J. Stott, Founder of Stottly Enterprises. "People are already running real work through it. Our job between now and launch is closing the gaps they're finding, not adding more before it's ready."

What's targeted for the 1.0 release

  • Guided company setup: a structured intake for starting a new company or documenting an existing one.
  • A persistent Project Manager that coordinates work, tracks the next required decision, and recommends the right workforce.
  • A living business plan that stays connected to decisions, goals, and company memory as the business changes.
  • Human and AI teams working from the same assignments, dependencies, and deadlines.
  • Governed execution, with the owner keeping approval authority over spending, publishing, and other consequential actions.
  • Connected business tools, so the systems a company already uses can plug into the same company record.

Preview access continues

The Agentic Forge preview build remains open while the team finishes the release. Early users are shaping the final feature set directly, and Stottly Enterprises is deliberately keeping that group small enough to act on what it hears.

Part of the Stottly Enterprises portfolio

Agentic Forge joins StottifAI, Gurilla Studios, CartCurios, and CryptoifAI as ventures built and operated by Stottly Enterprises. Each venture starts from a problem the company has seen repeatedly in client work and is built into a standalone product from there.

Availability

The Agentic Forge preview is available now at stottlyenterprises.com/ventures/agentic-forge. Version 1.0 is targeted for the fourth quarter of 2026; pricing and a confirmed launch date will be announced closer to release.
